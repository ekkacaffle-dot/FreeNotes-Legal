Creating notes and checklists

  1. In Notes, tap New note for text, or Checklist for a to-do list.
  2. Type a title and your text. FreeNotes saves as you type; the top of the editor shows “Saving…”, then “Saved on iPhone” (and “Synced to iCloud” when sync is on and the upload has finished).
  3. In a checklist, type in “Add an item” and tap Add. An item you typed but did not add is kept on your iPhone, even if you close the app, and is added when you tap Add, Notes (back) or Share.
  4. Tap the box to tick an item, × to remove it, and Pin note to keep a note at the top.

Folders and search

  • Folders tab: type a name and tap Create. Folder names must be different (ignoring capitals).
  • To move a note, open it and choose a folder from the folder menu under the title.
  • Removing a folder never deletes notes: they move to All Notes.
  • The search box searches titles, text and checklist items. Use All Notes, Pinned and Trash to filter.

Turning on iCloud Sync

  1. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings app with the Apple Account you want to use, and that iCloud is allowed for FreeNotes.
  2. In FreeNotes, go to Settings → iCloud Sync and turn on Sync with iCloud, then tap Turn On. Notes already on this iPhone are uploaded; nothing is deleted.
  3. Do the same on your other iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Notes from both are combined.
  4. Settings → iCloud Sync shows your iCloud account, the sync status, how many changes are waiting, and the last successful sync. Sync now (or Retry) syncs immediately.

Fixing iCloud Sync problems

  • “Can’t reach iCloud”: you’re offline or iCloud didn’t respond. Your notes are saved on the iPhone; they sync when you’re back online. Tap Retry to try now.
  • “Not signed in to iCloud”: sign in from the Settings app. Notes stay on the iPhone and sync resumes.
  • “A different iCloud account is signed in”: sync is paused so one person’s notes are never uploaded to another account. Choose Use this iCloud account (uploads this iPhone’s notes to it) or Turn off iCloud Sync (keeps them only on this iPhone).
  • “Your iCloud storage is full”: free up space or upgrade iCloud storage (see Apple’s iCloud storage guide), then tap Retry.
  • “FreeNotes data was deleted from iCloud”: sync was turned off and your notes are still on the iPhone. Turn sync on again to upload them.
  • “iCloud is restricted”: Screen Time or a device-management profile blocks iCloud for this app.
  • A change from another device hasn’t appeared: FreeNotes syncs when it opens, when you return to it, after you edit, and every minute while it is open. Open FreeNotes on both devices, or tap Sync now.
  • A note titled “(conflict copy)”: the same note was edited differently on two devices. Both versions were kept; copy what you need into one note and delete the other.

Using FreeNotes offline

Everything works without a connection: writing, checklists, folders, search, pinning, Recently Deleted and exports. Changes are saved on your iPhone straight away. With iCloud Sync on, they upload automatically when you are back online; until then the editor says “Saved on iPhone · Not synced yet”.

Exporting and importing backups

  1. Settings → Your notes → Export backup. Choose where to save the file (for example Files or iCloud Drive) in the share sheet.
  2. To restore, Settings → Your notes → Import backup, and choose a FreeNotes backup file (up to 128 MB).

Importing never removes or changes notes you already have. Notes that are already present are skipped, and notes that clash with different content are kept as separate copies. A backup contains your notes, folders and Recently Deleted notes as readable text, so store it somewhere private.

Restoring deleted notes

Deleted notes go to Recently Deleted and stay there until you remove them; they are not deleted automatically. In Notes, tap Trash, then Restore on the note. Delete forever cannot be undone, and with iCloud Sync on it also removes the note from your other devices. If you have a backup from before the deletion, you can import it.

Deleting data on your iPhone and in iCloud

  • One note: Trash, then Delete forever.
  • All notes on this iPhone: delete the FreeNotes app. With iCloud Sync off this is permanent unless you exported a backup; with it on, the iCloud copy remains.
  • All FreeNotes data in iCloud: Settings app → your name → iCloud → Storage (or Manage Account Storage) → FreeNotes → delete its data (wording varies by iOS version; see Apple’s guide). FreeNotes then turns sync off and keeps the notes on your iPhone.
  • To remove everything, delete the iCloud data as above and then delete the app on each device.

Reporting a problem

Email ekka.caffle@gmail.com. To help us without sharing anything private, include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version, and the FreeNotes version (Settings → FreeNotes);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
  • the exact wording of any message, and the status shown in Settings → iCloud Sync;
  • screenshots only if helpful — crop or cover any note text first.

Please do not send note contents, exported backups or “Save a copy of the stored data” files unless we ask and you are comfortable doing so: they contain your notes in readable form. We cannot see your notes or your iCloud data, so we cannot recover notes for you; we can help you recover them from your devices or backups.
