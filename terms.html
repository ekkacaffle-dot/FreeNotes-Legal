iCloud Sync availability

iCloud Sync is optional and provided through Apple’s iCloud service. It depends on Apple, on your Apple Account being signed in and allowed to use iCloud, on network access and on free iCloud storage. Sync can be delayed, paused or unavailable, for example when you are offline, signed out, your iCloud storage is full, or Apple’s service is down. FreeNotes always saves to your iPhone first and tells you when something has not synced.

Changes made on one device sync to your other devices, including deletions. When the same note is changed differently on two devices, FreeNotes keeps both versions and labels one “(conflict copy)”.

Backups and data loss

iCloud Sync is not a backup: a deletion on one device deletes the note everywhere. FreeNotes’ storage on your iPhone is not included in iPhone or iCloud backups, so with sync off, deleting the app or losing the iPhone loses your notes. Export a backup regularly if your notes matter to you, and check important notes and exported files before relying on them.

Device failure, storage problems, account changes, service outages, software defects or interrupted saves can cause lost changes or data loss.
