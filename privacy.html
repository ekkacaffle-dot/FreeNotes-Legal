Summary

  • Your notes are saved on your iPhone. There is no FreeNotes account and no FreeNotes server.
  • iCloud Sync is optional and off until you turn it on. When it is on, your notes are also stored in your own private iCloud account, run by Apple, so they can sync to your other devices.
  • The developer cannot see your notes, on your iPhone or in iCloud.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.

Information stored on your iPhone

FreeNotes stores the following in the app’s private storage on your iPhone, to make the app work:

  • note titles and text, checklist items and whether they are ticked;
  • folders, pinned status, created/updated dates, and notes in Recently Deleted;
  • your appearance setting (System, Light or Dark);
  • checklist items you have typed but not added yet;
  • if you use iCloud Sync, sync records that keep your devices in step, including a one-way fingerprint (hash) of your iCloud account identifier — never your Apple ID email, name or password.

This storage is excluded from iPhone backups and iCloud Backup and is protected by iOS data protection. If you have never turned on iCloud Sync, your notes exist only on that iPhone and in any backups you export yourself.

Optional iCloud Sync (Apple CloudKit)

iCloud Sync is off by default. You turn it on in FreeNotes under Settings → iCloud Sync, after a confirmation that explains your existing notes will be uploaded.

When iCloud Sync is on, FreeNotes stores your notes in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service, and keeps them in step with your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This means your notes are no longer only on your iPhone: copies are held by Apple in your iCloud account.

  • Synced: note titles and text, checklist items and ticks, folders, pinned status, dates, and whether a note is in Recently Deleted. Deleting a note forever deletes it from iCloud and your other devices.
  • Not synced: your appearance setting, checklist items you have not added yet, and your exported backup files.
  • Synced notes count toward your iCloud storage.

The developer does not run a server for this data and cannot view, access, change or delete what is in your private iCloud database.

Security: Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and on its servers. FreeNotes stores note content in CloudKit encrypted fields; Apple states that these fields are end-to-end encrypted when you turn on Advanced Data Protection for your Apple Account. FreeNotes does not add its own separate encryption. Apple describes the details in its iCloud data security overview.

Apple’s role

iCloud and CloudKit are operated by Apple. When iCloud Sync is on, Apple stores and processes your synced notes under its own terms and the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple also handles App Store downloads and purchases. Apple may give us App Store statistics and, only if you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, crash reports; FreeNotes does not add note contents to them. We do not receive your iCloud data from Apple.

iCloud accounts and signing out

FreeNotes remembers which iCloud account your notes were synced with (as a one-way fingerprint). If a different iCloud account signs in on the iPhone, sync pauses and nothing is uploaded to that account unless you choose “Use this iCloud account” and confirm. If you sign out of iCloud, your notes stay on the iPhone and sync resumes when the same account signs in again.

When iCloud Sync is turned off

  • Syncing stops. Every note currently on the iPhone stays there, including notes that were downloaded from iCloud.
  • Copies already in iCloud stay in your iCloud account until you delete them (see “Deleting your data”).
  • New changes stay only on the iPhone. If you turn sync on again with the same account, they are uploaded.

Exports and sharing you choose

Notes leave FreeNotes in other ways only when you choose to: by sharing a note, or by exporting a backup. The iOS share sheet and the place you choose (for example Files, Mail or another app) decide where the content goes and how it is handled.

Exported backups are readable JSON files containing your notes, folders and Recently Deleted notes. They are not password-protected by FreeNotes and do not include sync records or unfinished checklist items. Keep them somewhere safe. Temporary export files are removed from the app’s cache after sharing.

Deleting your data

  • One note: move it to Recently Deleted, then tap Delete forever. With iCloud Sync on, it is also removed from iCloud and your other devices.
  • Everything on the iPhone: delete the FreeNotes app. If iCloud Sync was off, this permanently removes your notes unless you exported a backup. If it was on, the iCloud copy remains.
  • Everything in iCloud: on your iPhone open Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage (or Manage Account Storage), choose FreeNotes if it is listed, and delete its data. The exact menu depends on your iOS version; see Apple’s guide to managing iCloud storage. FreeNotes then turns sync off and keeps the notes on your iPhone.
  • Exported or shared copies stay where you put them until you delete them there.

Deletion removes records from the app’s storage and iCloud; it is not a forensic secure-erasure guarantee. Because we never receive your notes, we cannot delete or recover them for you.

What FreeNotes does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no data sale or sharing for advertising.
  • No remote AI processing of your notes.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, location or photos.
  • No FreeNotes account and no FreeNotes server.

Children

FreeNotes is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. It has no social or messaging features. If a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.
